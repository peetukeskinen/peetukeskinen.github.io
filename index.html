Peetu Keskinen

Economist  |  Doctoral Researcher

I am an economist at the National Audit Office of Finland (NAOF) in the Fiscal Policy Monitoring Unit and a doctoral researcher in economics at the University of Helsinki. My work focuses on fiscal policy, macroeconometrics and sovereign debt.

Research interests

  • Macroeconomics
  • Fiscal policy
  • Sovereign debt
  • EU fiscal policy and fiscal rules

Methodologically, I work on macroeconomic modelling — Bayesian VARs, structural identification and nonlinear DSGE methods — and on the fiscal policy analysis that supports the EU fiscal framework.

Current work

My dissertation consists of three studies: on EU fiscal rules and multiple equilibria, on the mechanisms of fiscal consolidation, and on debt-dependent fiscal policy under sovereign default risk. Details are on the research page; earlier reports and articles are listed under publications.
