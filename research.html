Research

My dissertation, work in progress, consists of the three studies below. Working papers will be linked here once they are available.

Doctoral research

EU fiscal rules and multiple equilibria

Work in progress

I examine the reformed EU fiscal rules using a framework inspired by Lorenzoni & Werning (2019), focusing on whether the rules guarantee a unique low-debt equilibrium.

  • Draft not yet available

Mechanisms of fiscal consolidation

Work in progress

I estimate a Bayesian structural VAR with sign, zero and narrative restrictions to study how successful fiscal consolidations work and why their effects differ across episodes.

  • Draft not yet available

Debt-dependent fiscal policy and sovereign default risk

Work in progress

Using a nonlinear projection method and a model based on Bi, Shen & Yang (2016), I analyse how default risk alters fiscal policy effects in low- and high-debt regimes.

  • Draft not yet available
